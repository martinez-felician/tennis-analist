Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 27, 2026

What we collect

Your username and email, your password (stored only in scrambled form, so nobody can read it), and the match data you record: scores, statistics, notes, opponent and club names. Our server also keeps basic technical logs.

What we do not do

We do not show ads, we do not use analytics or tracking tools, and we do not sell your data. The fonts and scripts the app needs are served from our own server, not from third parties.

Cookies and browser storage

One cookie keeps you signed in. Your browser also stores your language, your match settings and a match in progress, so you do not lose it.

Payments

If you buy a paid plan, the payment is handled by Stripe. We never see or store your card number.

Email

We only send email when you ask to reset your password.

Where your data lives

Your data is stored on our hosting provider's servers and is only visible to you.

Your choices

You can edit your details, and delete your account and all of your matches, from your profile. For any other request about your data, contact us.

Children

Tennis Analyst is not for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you think a child under 13 has an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.

Contact

Questions about your privacy? Open an issue on the project page at github.com/martinez-felician/tennis-analist.
